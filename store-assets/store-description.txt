NAME
Berry Cream Theme

SUMMARY
A refined Chrome theme in dusty berry, rose cream, and soft blush, with gentle contrast for calm everyday browsing.

DESCRIPTION
Berry Cream Theme brings a soft berry touch to Chrome. A dusty berry-pink frame pairs with a rose-cream toolbar and active tab, while a pale blush new tab page keeps your browsing space light and calm. Muted plum text and icons keep the interface clear and easy to read.

The flat berry icon follows the same restrained rose palette. Solid colors throughout, with no wallpaper, no extra scripts, and no permissions required.

PROMO
A soft berry touch for every tab.
